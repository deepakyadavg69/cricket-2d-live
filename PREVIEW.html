3 · Awaaz kaise chalti hai (ducking)

Commentary ke waqt BGM apne aap dhima ho jata hai, phir wapas tez:
BGM 100% → commentary shuru → BGM 20% → commentary khatam → BGM 100%

Chauke/chhakke par awaaz tez aur unchi hoti hai (+24%/+16Hz), wicket par dhimi aur gehri (+10%/−6Hz), dot ball par bilkul sidhi (+8%). Isse awaaz robot jaisi nahi lagti.

4 · Delay kaise manage hoti hai (playout buffer)

Channel ~10 second piche chalega — par picture, scoreboard aur awaaz ek saath rahenge. Har event queue me jata hai aur theek 10 second baad, kam se kam 2.2 second ke faasle se nikalta hai.

Isse fayda: Cricbuzz ek baar me 3 ball de de to bhi commentary ek saath nahi ghus-ti — sab ek-ek karke, ek hi raftaar se aata hai. Measured: 31/32 balls par animation + SFX + awaaz bilkul ek saath (0.000s).
